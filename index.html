Who this is for

Investing was designed around the paycheck. Your life isn't.

If you drive, deliver, freelance, work for tips, run a side business or juggle shifts, your income goes up and down. Almost every investing app pretends it doesn't. HughMoney is being built for how you actually get paid, and for people who learn about money online but never had a tool that fit their life.

✕What most investing apps assume

  • A fixed paycheck lands every two weeks, same amount, same day.
  • Auto-deposit on a calendar date, whether or not you got paid.
  • Minimum balances and "missed contribution" warnings.
  • You already have thousands sitting around to start.
  • Advice straight from social media: hype first, your situation never.

✓What HughMoney is built around

  • Income that changes week to week. Good weeks invest more, slow weeks invest less or nothing.
  • Invest when money actually arrives: a percentage of each payout, or a tap when you have spare cash.
  • No minimum balance. Pause anytime, no penalty, no guilt.
  • Start from $1 with fractional shares of real companies and funds.
  • Plain-English guidance with no sponsored picks and no affiliate links.

How it will work

Three steps. Then it runs itself.

  1. 1

    Open an account in two minutes

    Sign up with your phone, verify your identity and link the account where your money lands. You must be 18+ and a US resident. No credit check, no proof of steady income.

  2. 2

    Tell us how you get paid

    Pick a rule that fits your income: a percentage of every payout, a fixed amount only on weeks you earn above a line you set, or simply a tap whenever you have spare cash. Then choose a ready-made portfolio or build your own.

  3. 3

    Go at your pace

    Good month? Top up. Slow month? Skip, pause, or pull cash back out of your individual account. No penalty, no streak to break, no guilt. Your plan bends with your life instead of breaking.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Is the app available now?

Not yet. We're building it right now. Join the waitlist and you'll be among the first invited to the beta, before the public launch.

When does it launch?

We'll open the beta to waitlist members first and roll out state by state after that. We'll email you the moment HughMoney is available where you live.

Do I need to know anything about investing?

No. You can start with a ready-made portfolio and learn as you go. Every term in the app has a one-line explanation right next to it.

My income changes every month. How does that work?

That's exactly who we're building for. Instead of a fixed deposit on a fixed date, you can invest a percentage of each payout as it arrives, set a floor below which nothing is invested, or just tap to move spare cash when you have it. Nothing is scheduled that your income can't cover.

Some months I can't invest anything. Is that a problem?

No. There is no minimum balance, no missed-contribution penalty and no streak to protect. Skip a month, or six, and pick up when things improve. Over years, an average of $20 a month at 7% from age 18 still grows to roughly $88,000 by 65. The years matter more than any single month.

Should I invest if I don't have an emergency fund yet?

Usually not all of it. With irregular income, a cash cushion for slow months comes first. The app will help you set a cushion goal and keep that part in cash, and only route what's above it into investments.

How is this different from the advice I see on TikTok or YouTube?

Creators get paid by sponsors, affiliate links and views, so the loudest advice is often the worst. HughMoney has no sponsored picks and no affiliate links. We explain what you own and why it moves, and we'll tell you plainly when something is risky.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Investments go up and down, and in any given year they can go down a lot. The reason to start early is that long time horizons have historically smoothed out those swings. We will never promise returns.

Can I take my money out when a slow month hits?

From your individual account, yes. Sell and withdraw any time; transfers usually arrive in 1–3 business days. Retirement accounts have tax rules about early withdrawals, and we flag those clearly before you confirm.

Who can open an account?

US residents aged 18 or older with a Social Security number and a US bank account. Custodial accounts for younger users are on our roadmap.
